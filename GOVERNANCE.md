# Governance

This document describes the governance and stewardship of the Meaningful Participation Reference Architecture (MPRA) and Meaningful Participation Protocol (MPP).

MPP is an open initiative stewarded by [Felixe](https://felixe.com). It originated from practical work exploring how digital ecosystems can better recognise, interpret and incentivise participation that advances their objectives.

The general-purpose architecture and protocol have been separated from any particular implementation and published openly so that they can be examined, challenged, implemented, adapted and improved independently.

## 1. Governance Principles

Governance of MPP is guided by the following principles.

### Open Access

Published versions of MPP and MPRA are available under the licence applicable to those versions.

Stewardship does not provide Felixe, its shareholders, employees, contractors or other associated parties with exclusive rights to use, implement or commercialise the published protocol.

### Transparent Development

Development takes place through this public repository wherever practical.

Material changes to the protocol should be visible through repository history, Issues, Pull Requests, releases or other public project records.

### Representation Before Interpretation

Governance of MPP concerns the structures through which Meaningful Participation can be represented. It does not give the project steward authority to determine what participation should mean or how it should be valued within independent ecosystems.

Those decisions remain with the ecosystems using or interpreting MPP.

### Open Implementation

Any individual or organisation may develop implementations, products, services, research or other work using or informed by MPP, subject to the applicable licence.

No implementation receives privileged status merely because of its relationship with the project steward.

### Forkability

The ability to continue, adapt and challenge open work is an important protection against capture.

Subject to the applicable licence, published versions may be forked and independently developed. If future stewardship of MPP takes a direction that participants or implementers do not support, they are not required to follow it.

## 2. Stewardship

Felixe currently acts as steward of MPP and MPRA.

Stewardship includes:

* maintaining the principal repository and project materials;
* reviewing proposed changes;
* maintaining coherence between the architecture, protocol specification, schemas, examples and explanatory materials;
* managing releases and versioning;
* maintaining project governance processes;
* recognising contributions where appropriate; and
* supporting discussion, testing and implementation of the work.

During the project's current stage of development, Felixe retains responsibility for determining what is incorporated into official versions of MPP and MPRA.

Open development does not require every proposed change to be accepted. Contributions may be discussed, revised, accepted or declined according to their contribution to the coherence, usefulness and objectives of the project.

## 3. Relationship Between Stewardship and Ownership

Stewardship should not be interpreted as exclusive ownership of the ideas represented by MPP or as a right to prevent others from implementing the published protocol.

Copyright and other intellectual-property rights in repository materials remain subject to the notices and licence applicable to those materials.

Contributors retain any rights they hold in their contributions except to the extent that those rights are licensed, assigned or otherwise governed by the applicable repository terms.

No contribution, participation record or recognition in `CONTRIBUTORS.md` should by itself be interpreted as transferring intellectual-property ownership.

## 4. Commercial Implementations

MPP and MPRA may inform commercial as well as nonprofit, academic, governmental and community applications.

Felixe may develop commercial products, services, consulting activities and implementations informed by or compatible with MPP. Other organisations may do the same.

A commercial implementation of MPP is separate from the protocol itself.

Product software, interfaces, proprietary algorithms, interpretation models, incentive mechanisms, datasets, business processes, brands, customer relationships and other implementation-specific assets do not become part of MPP merely because they use or implement the protocol.

Conversely, developing a commercial implementation does not confer ownership of, preferential access to or exclusive rights over the open protocol.

Where Felixe develops both MPP and a commercial implementation, changes to the open protocol should be evaluated according to the objectives and integrity of MPP rather than solely according to the interests of that implementation.

## 5. Public-Interest and Grant-Funded Development

MPP may receive support from philanthropic, nonprofit, public-interest or other funders.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise in the terms of a particular grant or contribution, funding the development of MPP does not provide the funder, Felixe or another party with exclusive ownership of the resulting published protocol.

Where grant funding is specifically provided for development of the open protocol or its public resources, Felixe should maintain sufficient project and financial records to distinguish that work from the development of proprietary commercial products.

Grant-funded outputs designated as open MPP or MPRA materials should be published through this repository or another publicly accessible project channel under the applicable open licence.

Funding relationships that could materially affect the development or governance of MPP should be disclosed where appropriate.

These provisions are intended to enable organisations to support development of MPP as open infrastructure without requiring Felixe or other organisations to relinquish their ability to develop separate commercial implementations.

## 6. Contributions

MPP welcomes contributions including:

* challenges to the architecture or protocol;
* proposed improvements;
* relevant research and standards;
* implementation experience;
* examples and counterexamples;
* technical contributions;
* critiques and edge cases; and
* proposed changes to project materials.

An Issue may be used to raise a question, critique or proposal. A Pull Request may be used to propose a specific change.

Contributing does not guarantee that a proposal will be incorporated into MPP.

A contribution may nevertheless constitute Meaningful Participation in the project even when the proposed change is not accepted.

Recognition of participation is described in [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md).

## 7. Decision-Making

MPP currently uses steward-led rather than consensus-based governance.

The steward considers contributions and proposed changes and determines what enters the official project materials and releases.

Decisions should take account of factors including:

* consistency with MPP's design principles;
* technical coherence;
* interoperability;
* implementation experience;
* evidence and relevant standards;
* backwards compatibility;
* privacy and participant agency;
* complexity introduced by the change; and
* whether the change belongs within the scope of MPP.

Significant or potentially contentious changes should, where practical, be discussed publicly before incorporation.

As participation in MPP develops, governance may evolve to include additional maintainers, technical reviewers, working groups or other forms of distributed stewardship.

Governance should not become more complex than the level of participation in the project requires.

## 8. Protocol Independence

MPP is intended to remain independent of any single implementation, sector, technology stack, identity system, credential system, scoring methodology, incentive mechanism or business model.

Implementations may extend MPP or combine it with other technologies and standards.

Such extensions do not become part of the MPP standard unless they are incorporated through the project's governance process.

Conformance with MPP should be determined by the applicable specification rather than by organisational affiliation with Felixe or another participant.

## 9. Changes to Governance

This governance model may evolve as MPP develops.

Material governance changes should be made transparently through this repository.

Changes to governance cannot retroactively revoke rights already granted under the licence applicable to a previously published version.

The objective is to preserve sufficient stewardship to maintain a coherent protocol while ensuring that published MPP infrastructure remains openly available for independent use, implementation, critique and development.
