It is also intended as a deliberately simple, human-readable representation of the Participation Records described by MPP. It is maintained manually and is **not** intended to demonstrate full protocol conformance.

## How Recognition Works

A contribution may be recognised when it materially helps to develop, improve, test, challenge, explain, govern, implement or advance MPP or an artefact maintained in this repository.

Relevant participation may include contributions to:

- the MPP specification;
- the *Making Meaningful Participation Portable* working paper;
- schemas and technical artefacts;
- examples and implementation guidance;
- terminology and conceptual development;
- testing and validation;
- governance and stewardship;
- documentation and repository structure;
- research and relevant standards;
- critique, review and challenge;
- implementation and interoperability; and
- adoption and ecosystem engagement.

A contribution does not need to result in accepted text, code or another repository change to be meaningful. A critique, test, question, alternative proposal or other contribution may itself materially improve the project even when the proposed change is not adopted.

### Commitment Classes

Commitment Classes are assigned according to the MPP specification. A single contribution may involve one or more of **Effort, Capital, Standing and Knowledge**.

Public identification of a Participant does **not** automatically constitute a commitment of Standing. Standing should be recorded only where the Participant's recognised position, authority, accountability, reputation or professional standing materially contributes to the significance of the participation.

A useful practical test is whether the contribution would have materially different significance if the Participant were anonymous. If the Participant's recognised position, authority, accountability or reputation is itself part of the value committed through the participation, Standing may apply.

### Evidence

Where practical, recognised participation should link to evidence such as an Issue, Pull Request, commit, review, document, discussion or other supporting material. Early contributions that occurred outside GitHub may use a descriptive evidence reference until more structured records are available.

Evidence supports the representation of participation; it does not by itself determine whether that participation is meaningful.

### Identifiers and Privacy

Public identifiers such as GitHub profiles, LinkedIn profiles, ORCID identifiers, organisational profiles or other appropriate references may be included with the Participant's agreement.

Identifiers are used to distinguish Participants and make attribution clearer. They should not be interpreted as credentials, evidence of participation, verification of a claim, or a commitment of Standing unless the relevant record explicitly establishes that role.

Participants may request correction or removal of personal identifiers or other information associated with their entries.

## Contributing

We welcome challenges to the ideas presented here, suggested improvements, relevant research and standards, examples from different ecosystems, implementation experience and proposed changes to repository materials.

You can contribute by:

- **opening an Issue** to raise a question, critique, suggestion or area for discussion;
- **submitting a Pull Request** to propose a specific change; or
- **joining an existing Issue or Pull Request** to contribute to an ongoing discussion.

Proposed changes do not automatically become part of MPP. Contributions may be discussed, revised, accepted or declined by the project maintainers.

Where a contribution constitutes Meaningful Participation, it may be recognised in this file whether or not the proposed change is ultimately accepted.

## Stewardship and Future Development

Recognition is currently maintained manually by the project steward.

As MPP develops, this repository may move towards generating machine-readable Participation Records for recognised contributions. The intention is for the repository to become an increasingly complete reference implementation of the protocol it develops while preserving a clear human-readable contribution history.

Recognition in this file does not imply endorsement of MPP, agreement with every part of the project, authorship of the specification or working paper, or any transfer of intellectual property beyond the terms applicable to the relevant contribution.

## Corrections

If you believe a contribution has been omitted, incorrectly described, incorrectly classified or attributed to the wrong Participant, please open an Issue so the record can be reviewed and corrected.
